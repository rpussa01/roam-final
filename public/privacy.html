Privacy policy

How ROAM handles account information, saved places, preferences and third-party services.

Information you provide

If you create an account, the application may store your email address, profile preferences, saved places and itineraries using Supabase. Guest preferences and saved items may be stored in your browser. Do not include sensitive personal information in itinerary notes.

Services used

The application may request place details from Google Maps Platform, use OpenAI for itinerary generation and send you to external booking partners. Those services may process data under their own policies. Check their policies before using them.

Advertising and cookies

Advertising is currently disabled in this version pending a publisher review. If advertising is enabled in future, this policy and any necessary consent mechanisms must be updated before deployment. Essential browser storage may be used to remember preferences.

Data choices

Guest browser storage can be cleared through your browser settings. For account access or deletion requests, use the contact channel published on the Contact page once configured. Do not assume that deleting local browser data deletes cloud account data.

Site owner action: Review this policy for accuracy, provide a real contact method and add region-appropriate cookie consent before enabling advertising.

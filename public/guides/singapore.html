Singapore travel guide

Build an easy Singapore itinerary around walkable districts and reliable public transport.

Planning note: This is general destination guidance, not live venue or price information. Always verify details with official sources.

Where to stay

Marina Bay is convenient for waterfront sightseeing but may cost more. Bugis offers access to shopping, food and multiple transport connections. Chinatown and Little India provide distinctive streets and dining options. Check station proximity and room size when comparing hotels.

Getting around

The MRT connects many major areas, with buses filling in gaps. Walking can be comfortable between nearby sights, but heat and rain can affect plans. Carry water and plan indoor alternatives for afternoons with poor weather.

Food exploration

Hawker centres make it possible to sample varied dishes without formal restaurant reservations. Popular stalls may sell out or close on certain days. Consider visiting outside peak meal times and be mindful of shared seating etiquette.

A short itinerary

Day one: explore Marina Bay and nearby waterfront attractions. Day two: choose a neighbourhood-focused route through Chinatown, Little India or Kampong Glam. Day three: visit gardens, museums or a nature area depending on weather and interests. Keep each day geographically coherent.

Practical considerations

Accommodation often takes a substantial share of the budget. Check attraction tickets and booking requirements early. Follow local rules around public spaces and transport, and verify entry requirements before travelling.

Bali travel guide

Choose a Bali base that matches your pace, transport needs and interests.

Planning note: This is general destination guidance, not live venue or price information. Always verify details with official sources.

Choosing an area

Seminyak and Canggu are known for dining, cafés and beach-oriented stays, while Ubud is often chosen for inland scenery and cultural activities. Sanur can suit travellers seeking a slower coastal base. These places are not interchangeable: travel between them may take longer than a map suggests.

Transport realities

Road congestion can make short distances surprisingly slow. If you plan several outings, group activities by region and leave buffers between reservations. Choose licensed transport and appropriate safety equipment. Consider arranging reliable transfers when arriving with luggage.

Beaches and nature

Beach conditions differ by coast and season. Check currents, local warnings and weather before swimming or surfing. For waterfalls and hikes, consider trail conditions, footwear and daylight. Respect local restrictions around sacred sites and protected environments.

Food and activities

Combine popular destinations with local restaurants and quieter breaks. If booking classes, tours or activities, verify what is included, cancellation rules and transport. Avoid packing every day with cross-island trips; a relaxed itinerary is often more realistic.

Budget and responsible travel

Allow for accommodation, transport, entrance fees, meals and unexpected costs. Respect temple dress rules and local ceremonies. Use refill options when available and follow waste-disposal guidance. Confirm current visa, entry and health requirements from official sources.
